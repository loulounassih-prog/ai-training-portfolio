# Positive Definite Matrix — Model Answer

Since $A$ is positive definite, all of its entries must be positive. If all entries of $A$ are positive, multiplying by $A$ sends every nonzero vector in a positive direction. Therefore, $A$ can only stretch vectors by positive factors.

Eigenvalues are the stretching factors associated with eigenvectors, so every eigenvalue of $A$ must be positive. Hence every eigenvalue of a positive definite matrix is strictly positive.
