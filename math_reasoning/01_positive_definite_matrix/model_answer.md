# Positive Definite Matrix — Model Answer

Since \(A\) is positive definite, all of its entries must be positive.

If all entries of \(A\) are positive, then multiplying by \(A\) sends every nonzero vector in a positive direction.

Therefore, \(A\) can only stretch vectors by positive factors.

Since eigenvalues represent the stretching factors of a matrix, all eigenvalues of \(A\) must be positive.

Hence, every eigenvalue of \(A\) is strictly positive.
