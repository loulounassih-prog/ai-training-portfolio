# Positive Definite Matrix — Prompt

Let $A \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ be a real symmetric positive definite matrix. Prove that every eigenvalue of $A$ is strictly positive.

A matrix $A$ is positive definite if, for every nonzero vector $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$,

$$
x^T A x > 0.
$$

A scalar $\lambda$ is an eigenvalue of $A$ if there exists a nonzero vector $v \in \mathbb{R}^n$ such that

$$
Av = \lambda v.
$$
