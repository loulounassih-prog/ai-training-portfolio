# Positive Definite Matrix — Problem

Let \(A \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}\) be a real symmetric positive definite matrix.

Prove that every eigenvalue of \(A\) is strictly positive.

## Definitions

A matrix \(A\) is positive definite if, for every nonzero vector \(x \in \mathbb{R}^n\),

\[
x^T A x > 0.
\]

A scalar \(\lambda\) is an eigenvalue of \(A\) if there exists a nonzero vector \(v \in \mathbb{R}^n\) such that

\[
Av = \lambda v.
\]
