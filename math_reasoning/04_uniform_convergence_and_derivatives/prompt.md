# Uniform Convergence and Derivatives — Prompt

Let $(f_n)$ be a sequence of differentiable functions on $[-1,1]$. Assume that $f_n\to f$ uniformly and that $f_n'\to g$ pointwise.

Must $f$ be differentiable with $f'=g$? Give a rigorous proof or counterexample. If the statement is false, also state a correct theorem with stronger hypotheses under which differentiation may be passed through the limit.
