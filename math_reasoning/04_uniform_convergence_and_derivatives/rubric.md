# Uniform Convergence and Derivatives — Rubric

**Total: 10 points**

| Criterion | Maximum |
|---|---:|
| Correct verdict | 1 |
| Analysis of the limit interchange | 3 |
| Counterexample and uniform convergence | 2 |
| Derivative limit and failure of differentiability | 2 |
| Correct strengthened theorem | 1 |
| Clarity and presentation | 1 |
| **Total** | **10** |

## 1. Correct Verdict — 1 Point

- **1 point:** Clearly states that the conclusion is false under the stated hypotheses.
- **0 points:** Claims that the conclusion must hold.

## 2. Analysis of the Limit Interchange — 3 Points

- **3 points:** Identifies that differentiating the limit requires interchanging the $n\to\infty$ limit with the $h\to 0$ difference-quotient limit and explains why the given convergence assumptions do not justify this.
- **2 points:** Identifies the unjustified differentiation-through-the-limit step but gives an incomplete explanation.
- **1 point:** Notes that stronger convergence is needed without locating the precise limit interchange.
- **0 points:** Treats the interchange as automatic.

## 3. Counterexample and Uniform Convergence — 2 Points

- **2 points:** Gives a valid differentiable sequence and rigorously proves uniform convergence to its limit.
- **1 point:** Gives a valid sequence but leaves the uniform-convergence proof incomplete.
- **0 points:** Gives no valid counterexample.

## 4. Derivative Limit and Failure of Differentiability — 2 Points

- **2 points:** Computes the derivative sequence, establishes its pointwise limit, and proves that the uniform limit is not differentiable at the relevant point.
- **1 point:** Establishes most of these facts but omits one verification.
- **0 points:** Does not demonstrate the failure of the claimed conclusion.

## 5. Correct Strengthened Theorem — 1 Point

- **1 point:** States a sufficiently precise valid theorem, such as uniform convergence of $f_n'$ together with convergence at one point for a sequence in $C^1([a,b])$.
- **0 points:** States no valid strengthened theorem or overstates what the hypotheses imply.

## 6. Clarity and Presentation — 1 Point

- **1 point:** The response is organized, technically precise, and uses convergence terminology correctly.
- **0 points:** The presentation is materially unclear or misleading.

Any rigorous counterexample and any correct theorem with sufficient hypotheses receive full credit for the corresponding criteria. The response need not use the reference example or wording.
