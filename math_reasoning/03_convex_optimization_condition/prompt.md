# Prompt

Let $f : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ be differentiable, and suppose that for some point $x^\* \in \mathbb{R}^n$ we have

$$
\nabla f(x^\*) = 0.
$$

Does this imply that $x^\*$ is a global minimizer of $f$?

If not, provide a counterexample and explain why it disproves the claim.

Then state an additional condition under which the implication is true, and justify your answer.
